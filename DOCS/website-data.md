## Objectives
- **Smarter GPU Cluster Management**: Encourage hands-on engagement with the real-world challenge of orchestrating GPU and CPU clusters — the compute layer that powers every AI workload.
- **Spark Innovation & Startup Potential**: Companies like Rafay and Mirantis have built businesses orchestrating GPU clusters, but the intelligent layer on top is still wide open — a real gap and a great problem for hackers.
- **Build a Strong AI Community at DIU**: Unite DIU students and alumni around cutting-edge AI themes, fostering collaboration, mentorship, and a culture of innovation within the university ecosystem.
- **Build the Smart Layer for Compute**: Challenge participants to build the intelligent layer that decides what runs where, predicts failures, and cuts idle GPU costs — making AI compute faster, cheaper, and more reliable.

---

## Hackathon Track: AI for Cluster Intelligence
Teams can tackle any of these angles or combine them:
1. **Smart Scheduling**: Using ML to place workloads and pack GPUs efficiently.
2. **Predictive Operations**: Spotting failures and anomalies before they happen.
3. **Cost Optimization**: Cutting idle GPU spend automatically.

## Hackathon Phases
The hackathon is divided into two main phases:

### Phase 1 — Preliminary (Online)
Submit your project idea, proposed architecture, and potential impact. This round is conducted entirely online.

#### Deliverables
- **Concept Note**: Problem Statement + Solution
- **Presentation Slides**: Maximum 10 Slides
- **Video Presentation**: 3–5 Minutes

#### Judging Criteria (100 Marks Total)
- **Innovation & Creativity**: 40% (40 Marks)
- **Idea Concept & Technical Analysis**: 30% (30 Marks)
- **Real-world Impact**: 20% (20 Marks)
- **Presentation Quality**: 10% (10 Marks)

#### Team Selection
- Top **50 teams** will be selected to advance to the Final Round.
- **10 teams** will be placed on the Waiting List.

---

### Phase 2 — Final Round (On-site)
**Duration**: 8 Hours  
Selected finalist teams will compete on-site, building their prototypes live and pitching them to a panel of industry experts and faculty judges.

#### Judging Criteria (100 Marks Total)
- **Innovation**: 20% (20 Marks)
- **Technical Complexity**: 20% (20 Marks)
- **Real-world Impact**: 20% (20 Marks)
- **Scalability & Sustainability**: 15% (15 Marks)
- **Presentation & Demo**: 15% (15 Marks)
- **Team Collaboration**: 10% (10 Marks)
