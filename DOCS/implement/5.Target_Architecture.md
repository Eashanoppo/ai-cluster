# 02. Target Architecture

## 2.1 Architecture Vision

The AI Cloud Operating System (AIOS) follows a **modular, distributed, event-driven, and cloud-native architecture** designed to autonomously manage AI infrastructure at scale.

The platform separates responsibilities across presentation, application, orchestration, intelligence, infrastructure, observability, and data layers, allowing each subsystem to evolve independently while remaining tightly integrated through well-defined APIs and event pipelines.

At the center of the architecture is **processors.py**, the autonomous decision engine that continuously observes, analyzes, predicts, decides, executes, validates, and learns from infrastructure operations.

# 2.6 Architectural Principles

AIOS follows these principles:

* Cloud-native architecture.
* Modular services with clear responsibilities.
* Event-driven communication.
* API-first backend.
* Autonomous decision-making through `processors.py`.
* Explainable AI decisions.
* Digital Twin validation before major actions.
* Local machine learning without external AI APIs.
* Secure-by-design with JWT and RBAC.
* Horizontally scalable infrastructure.
* High observability and auditability.
* Human oversight for policy-defined critical actions.

# 2.8 Functional Requirements

The target architecture shall:

* Use **Next.js 16.2.1** as the frontend for the simulator, dashboards, Digital Twin, and visualization.
* Use **Django** as the backend for REST APIs, WebSockets, authentication, business logic, and orchestration.
* Centralize all autonomous decision-making within **processors.py**.
* Use **Kubernetes** for container orchestration and **Ray** for distributed AI workload execution.
* Collect infrastructure telemetry through **Prometheus** and **NVIDIA DCGM**.
* Store operational, telemetry, learning, and audit data in **PostgreSQL**, with **Redis** supporting caching, sessions, and messaging.
* Validate significant infrastructure decisions using the **Digital Twin Engine** before execution whenever feasible.
* Support modular expansion, horizontal scalability, high availability, and secure, explainable autonomous operations suitable for both hackathon demonstrations and future enterprise deployment.
