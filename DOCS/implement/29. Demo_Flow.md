# 29. Demo Flow

## 29.1 Purpose

The **Demo Flow** defines the complete end-to-end demonstration of the AI Cloud Operating System (AIOS) for hackathon judges, investors, researchers, and enterprise stakeholders.

Its objective is to showcase that AIOS is **not simply a monitoring dashboard**, but an **autonomous AI-powered Cloud Operating System** capable of intelligently managing Kubernetes clusters, GPUs, distributed AI workloads, failures, scaling, cost optimization, and infrastructure decisions with minimal human intervention.

The demonstration emphasizes **autonomous decision-making**, **real-time observability**, **predictive intelligence**, **self-healing infrastructure**, and **explainable AI decisions**.

---

# 29.24 Functional Requirements

The Demo Flow shall:

* Demonstrate the complete lifecycle of an autonomous AI workload from simulator input to execution and optimization.
* Showcase seamless integration between the Next.js frontend, Django backend, `processors.py`, Kubernetes, Ray, Prometheus, NVIDIA DCGM, the Digital Twin, and the Learning Engine.
* Highlight autonomous scheduling, predictive failure detection, live migration, capacity management, cost optimization, and self-learning.
* Provide real-time visualizations, explainable AI decisions, and interactive dashboards suitable for technical and non-technical audiences.
* Require human intervention only for policy-defined high-impact actions, reinforcing AIOS as a safe, explainable, and autonomous AI Cloud Operating System.
