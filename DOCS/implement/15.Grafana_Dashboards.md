# 15. Grafana Dashboards

## 15.1 Purpose

**Grafana** is the **Visualization and Operational Intelligence Layer** of the AI Cloud Operating System (AIOS).

Its responsibility is to transform raw telemetry, infrastructure metrics, AI workload statistics, predictions, anomaly reports, and autonomous decisions into interactive real-time dashboards that provide complete visibility into the entire AI cloud infrastructure.

Grafana serves two primary audiences:

* **AI Control Plane (`processors.py`)** by exposing operational insights and validating autonomous decisions.
* **Human Operators and Hackathon Judges** by providing intuitive, explainable, and interactive visualizations of the platform's behavior.

Grafana does **not** make operational decisions. It visualizes the state of the system while processors.py remains the sole autonomous decision-making authority.

---

# 15.2 Objectives

The Grafana integration shall:

* Visualize the complete AI infrastructure.
* Display real-time GPU telemetry.
* Monitor Kubernetes and Ray clusters.
* Display workload execution.
* Visualize anomaly detection results.
* Explain autonomous decisions.
* Show predictive analytics.
* Display cost and power savings.
* Monitor SLA compliance.
* Provide executive-level operational dashboards.

# 15.24 Functional Requirements

The Grafana integration shall:

* Provide real-time visualization for every major AIOS component.
* Display telemetry from Prometheus, NVIDIA DCGM, Kubernetes, Ray, Node Exporter, and AIOS services.
* Present infrastructure, workload, prediction, anomaly, business, and executive dashboards.
* Visualize autonomous decisions made by processors.py with supporting explanations and confidence scores.
* Support interactive filtering, drill-down analysis, and historical replay.
* Refresh dashboards with configurable intervals appropriate to each metric category.
* Provide role-based dashboard views for administrators, operators, business users, and hackathon judges.
* Serve as the primary visualization platform of AIOS while remaining read-only; all operational decisions continue to be made exclusively by the AI Control Plane (`processors.py`).
